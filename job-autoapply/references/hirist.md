# Hirist

Tech-only board aimed at experienced engineers (roughly 5+ years). Best for senior profiles. Default cap 15/day (hard max 30).

## Pre-flight
`https://www.hirist.tech/` (older links may use hirist.com). Login wall → stop.

## Discover
Search by role and location; filter by experience. Card fields: title, company, experience range, location, posted date, job id from the URL.

## Apply
Flow is Naukri-like: **Apply** → sometimes recruiter questions → submit → "Applied" state. Reuse the Naukri question mapping (`references/naukri.md`, table at bottom). Skip roles whose minimum experience exceeds the profile.
